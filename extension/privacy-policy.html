Team Brain — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

What we collect

Team Brain collects URLs, page titles, and time spent on web pages while the extension is active. This data is used solely to identify professional skills and learnings.

What we filter out

The extension automatically discards activity on social media, email, shopping, entertainment, news, and banking websites. This data is never stored or transmitted.

Where data is stored

All data stays on your local machine. Browsing signals are stored in Chrome's local storage and periodically sent to a companion process running on localhost. No data is sent to external servers except for a daily analysis call to the Anthropic API, which receives only filtered, work-related browsing signals (URLs and page titles).

Data sharing

Your browsing data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The only external transmission is to the Anthropic API for skill extraction analysis.

Your control

You can pause tracking at any time using the extension popup. You can uninstall the extension to stop all data collection. All locally stored data is deleted when the extension is removed.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact your team administrator.
